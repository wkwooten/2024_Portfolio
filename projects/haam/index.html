1. Overview

Project Summary

HAAM is a commercial mobile application being developed to make tracking Congress simple and engaging for everyday citizens. Currently in active development with two software engineers, we're building it using Flutter for both iOS and Android.

Status

In development (2024)

Platform

iOS & Android

Technology

Flutter

Problem & Solution

Existing legislative platforms are complex and fail to engage citizens. HAAM aims to provide unprecedented transparency through personalized tracking of bills and representatives with a modern, accessible interface.
