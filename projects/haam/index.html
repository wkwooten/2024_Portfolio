1. Overview

Background & Project Scope

Haam is an ambitious project aiming to create the most comprehensive Congress tracking application on the market. This ongoing initiative focuses on making complex legislative information accessible and engaging for everyday citizens.

Timeline:

Ongoing development (2024-Present)

Key Deliverables:

- Comprehensive user research

- Complete design system

- High-fidelity interactive prototype

- User testing and iteration documentation

The Problem

Existing legislative information platforms are often complex, inaccessible, and fail to engage everyday citizens, leading to a disconnect between the public and the legislative process. Americans want to understand and engage with Congress, but current solutions lack user-centered design and modern engagement features.

Our Solution

Haam is being designed as a comprehensive platform that makes tracking Congress simple, informative, and engaging. The application will provide unprecedented transparency into legislative processes, personalized tracking of bills and representatives, and novel engagement features to help users connect with the democratic process.

2. The Challenge

Design Challenges

Making complex legislative data accessible and engaging to everyday citizens while providing unprecedented transparency into the workings of Congress presents several key challenges:

  • Simplifying complex legislative processes and terminology without losing accuracy
  • Creating engagement mechanisms that encourage continued use
  • Designing data visualizations that make legislative information digestible
  • Building personalization features that allow users to focus on issues they care about
  • Balancing comprehensive data with a clean, approachable interface
