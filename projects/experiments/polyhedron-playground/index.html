Interactive Polyhedron Playground

Click and drag the polyhedrons to move them around. Release to throw them and watch them bounce off the invisible walls.

The polyhedrons respond to physics and will interact with each other when they collide.

This playground uses Three.js for rendering and Cannon.js for physics simulation.
